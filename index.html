01 / WHY FORESTS MATTER

Nature doesn't need us.
We need nature.

Forests regulate our climate, store carbon, protect soil, support water cycles and sustain countless species. Yet deforestation and degradation continue to threaten these vital ecosystems.

Protecting forests is not just an environmental choice. It is an investment in a healthier, more resilient future for all.
